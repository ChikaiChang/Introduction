About

我是常淇开，北京理工大学光电学院光学工程硕士研究生，方向为信息光子技术。本科就读智能感知工程，保研在北京理工大学继续攻读研究生，现在在 光操控与测量技术实验室 做空芯光纤里的纳米颗粒光学捕获、成像与事件相机测速。

I am Qikai Chang, an M.Eng. student in Optical Engineering at the School of Optics and Photonics, Beijing Institute of Technology, focusing on information photonics. I studied Intelligent Perception Engineering as an undergraduate and stayed at BIT for graduate school via recommended admission. I now work in the Laboratory of Optical Manipulation & Metrology on optical trapping, imaging, and event-camera velocimetry of nanoparticles in hollow-core fibers.

事件相机跟踪空芯光纤内纳米颗粒

Event-camera tracking of nanoparticles in hollow-core fiber

第一作者 · ACP 2026 · 2025.03 – 2026.07

First author · ACP 2026 · Mar 2025 – Jul 2026

空芯光纤内约 150 nm 颗粒侧向散射弱、像面畸变大，传统质心跟踪容易丢帧。我搭建事件相机成像与测速系统，用离焦三角孔径把光斑做成三角形，再以前序帧速度建模板、在预测位置匹配，随后做轴向投影差分测速。相较质心基线，有效帧比例由 84.1% 提升到 100%，速度周期与多普勒测速一致。

Lateral scatter from ~150 nm particles in a hollow-core fiber is weak and distorted, so centroid tracking drops frames. I built an event-camera imaging and velocimetry setup: a defocused triangular aperture turns the spot into a triangle; a two-step tracker predicts from prior speed, matches a template, then estimates velocity from axial projection differences. Effective frames rose from 84.1% to 100% versus the centroid baseline, and the velocity period matches Doppler velocimetry.

大气细颗粒物光学操纵与高灵敏表征

Optical manipulation and characterization of fine particles

项目负责人 · 北京市自然科学基金“启研”计划 · 2024.10 – 2025.09

PI · Beijing Natural Science Foundation Qiyan Program · Oct 2024 – Sep 2025

研究空芯光纤内亚微米颗粒的光学捕获与表征，使用脉冲/连续双光系统，由运动颗粒散射光的多普勒频移直接读取轴向速度，提高时空分辨率。项目形成期刊论文、会议论文与专利各一项。

I led a project on trapping and characterizing sub-micron particles in hollow-core fiber, using pulsed and continuous dual-beam illumination and Doppler shifts in scattered light for axial velocity. The project produced a journal paper, a conference paper, and a patent.

非接触式酒精浓度测量

Non-contact alcohol concentration sensing

项目负责人 · 全国大学生光电设计竞赛国家级一等奖 · 2023.06 – 2023.08

Lead · National First Prize, Optoelectronic Design Contest · Jun–Aug 2023

设计 1300 nm 光学检测系统，用机器学习做信号分析与温度漂移补偿，实现非接触酒精检测，误差小于 0.5%。

Designed a 1300 nm optical sensing system with machine-learning signal analysis and temperature-drift compensation. Non-contact alcohol measurement error stayed below 0.5%.

冬奥高速多场景智能跟踪摄影

High-speed tracking camera system for the Winter Olympics

嵌入式模块负责人 · 2021.10 – 2022.06

Embedded-system lead · Oct 2021 – Jun 2022

基于 STM32 与 PID 做全局相机引导，结合 YOLOv5 捕获高速目标，多云台协同减少后期工作量；系统用于 2022 冬奥实景拍摄。

Built an STM32 + PID global camera guidance module with YOLOv5 target capture. Multi-gimbal coordination cut post-production workload; the system was used for on-site filming at the 2022 Winter Olympics.
